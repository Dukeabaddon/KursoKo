## Common landing sections (industry)

1. **Hero** — outcome headline + time/free cues  
2. **Trust strip** — ~10 min · Free · No signup · Private  
3. **What is RIASEC?** — 6 types explained simply  
4. **How it works** — 3 steps (answer → profile → explore)  
5. **Why us** — validated, private, instant  
6. **FAQ** — who is it for, accuracy, retake, account  
7. **Final CTA**

KursoKo landing now follows this structure.

## Messaging that converts (from competitors)

| Theme | Example phrasing |
|-------|------------------|
| No wrong answers | “Pick what you’d enjoy, not what you’re trained for” (Truity) |
| Any life stage | “Starting out, switching careers, or recovering from burnout” (JobCannon) |
| Evidence | “Same framework as O*NET / career counselors” |
| Low friction | “No email, no paywall after questions” |
| Outcome | “See which environments fit you” not “get your personality type” |

**Avoid:** MBTI confusion, “scientific personality”, corporate SaaS tone, SHS-only unless PH-specific block.
